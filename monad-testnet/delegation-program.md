# Monad Testnet and the Validator Delegation Program

> Reviewed 2026-09-15 against the [official Monad Validator Delegation Program](https://docs.monad.xyz/node-ops/validator-delegation-program). This is an evidence-requirements reference, not an application, allocation, staking, or validator-operation workflow.

## Testnet relationship to the published program

The official Validator Delegation Program (VDP) page lists continuous Monad testnet operation, 98% uptime, and four weeks of testnet intake evidence among its published criteria. It also says the application form is forthcoming. This Hub therefore does **not** claim an open testnet application, a separate testnet grant, guaranteed allocation or delegation, or Foundation eligibility.

The page header is dated April 22 while the body includes an April 24 stage-two adjustment. Treat it as a dated policy snapshot and recheck the official page before relying on any criterion.

## What the dated policy says

The reviewed policy also describes KYC/KYB and metrics access, plus a delegation-cap policy that is permanently 10% and temporarily 15% after the observed April 24 adjustment (superseding a previous 20% figure). The temporary value is not a permanent rule.

This testnet page does not convert a node process, RPC response, uptime display, or self-reported metric into program qualification. The official policy and a complete evidence window remain the authority.

## Reusable program-review record

Any network can use this Node Ops module only when it publishes a dated authoritative policy. Record the authority URL, review date, target network, eligibility window, stated caps, evidence owner, application status, and the conditions that remain unpublished. Keep policy facts separate from locally observed performance and never infer an approval from a public uptime chart.

Before an operator acts, verify the current policy, application availability, validator identity, evidence window, and privacy/legal handling through the authoritative channel. Wallet connection, transaction signing, stake movement, KYC/KYB submission, metrics sharing, or third-party contact remain explicit operator decisions outside this Hub.
