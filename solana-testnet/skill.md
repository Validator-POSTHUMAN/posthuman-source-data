Use a testnet-specific target and independent testnet RPC when checking voting. Never run the same identity on two hosts, preserve tower continuity during a cutover, and verify vote and root advancement externally. The skill does not authorize transactions, key movement, data deletion or public RPC exposure. Supply your own operator inventory; no POSTHUMAN hosts, keys or credentials are published here.
